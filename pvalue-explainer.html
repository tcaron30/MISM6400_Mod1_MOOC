A p-value tells you how likely your results are due to random chance. When it's below 0.05, you can be confident the effect is real. On the next page, you'll use exactly that logic to interpret your free shipping experiment.

Remember: statistical significance tells you the effect is real, not that it's large enough to act on. That's the next question you'll need to answer.

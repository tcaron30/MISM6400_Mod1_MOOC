One way to think about it
1
Repeat customer rate
When this changes, you know exactly what to do: adjust your loyalty strategy, pricing, or customer experience. It connects directly to long-term revenue.
2
Upsell success rate
A drop here signals a specific problem: staff training, menu layout, or point-of-sale prompts. Clear cause, clear action.
3
Peak hour percentage
Useful for staffing decisions, but it tends to be stable. Less likely to shift week to week, so it drives fewer day-to-day decisions.
4
Average transaction value
Important context, but on its own it doesn't tell you why it changed or what to do. It's more useful as a supporting metric than a primary KPI.
There's no single right answer here. The best KPI depends on your business priorities. What matters is that you can connect each metric to a specific decision.

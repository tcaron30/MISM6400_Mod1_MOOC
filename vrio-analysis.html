VRIO Analysis

Go back to the company you analyzed earlier and pick one of the competitive strengths you identified. You're going to test whether that strength can create lasting advantage or just a temporary edge.

Step 1: Describe Your Capability

Describe it in one sentence—be as specific as you can.

Step 2: Test Each VRIO Criterion

Valuable Does this capability clearly help customers or reduce costs?
Rare Do most competitors lack this capability?
Inimitable Would it take competitors years to copy this?
Organized Does the company have systems to fully use this strength?

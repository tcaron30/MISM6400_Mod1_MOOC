Complete

Total website visitors
Problem: volume without behavior
Visitors mean nothing if they leave without buying. A better metric: shopping cart abandonment rate or conversion rate. Both tell you what visitors actually did.
Social media followers
Problem: no connection to business outcomes
Follower counts feel impressive but don't drive revenue or retention. A better metric: customer acquisition cost from social channels. Did those followers become customers?
Number of transactions
Problem: volume without context
More transactions could mean growth, or it could mean heavy discounting and one-time buyers. A better metric: repeat purchase rate within 90 days. It tells you whether customers are coming back.
Total revenue
Problem: a lagging indicator with no actionability
Revenue can climb while the business deteriorates. A better metric: customer lifetime value. It connects revenue to the long-term health of the customer relationship.
The pattern: Every metric on ShopFlow's dashboard measured volume or output. None of them measured quality, behavior, or what customers would do next. A dashboard full of numbers that only go up isn't useful. The metrics that matter are the ones that tell you when something is wrong before it shows up in revenue.
